Divisions

Four divisions. Every analyst researches something real, presents it, and defends it in front of people who do this for a living. Here is what each division covers and how the year runs.

  • Equities
  • FICC
  • Macro
  • Quant

Equities

Research, analyse and pitch stocks as a team, then run them in a portfolio.

Pick the stocks, argue for them, and run them against a benchmark. Covering developed and emerging markets.

Developed Markets

Two division heads covering established economies such as the US, UK and Europe.

Emerging Markets

Two division heads covering fast-growing economies such as India, Brazil and Southeast Asia.

What you’ll do

  • Research, analyse and pitch stocks as a team.
  • Build and run a mock portfolio, with weekly trades posted on our website against a benchmark.
  • Present your team’s performance at bi-weekly market meetings.

What we’ll teach you

  • The fundamentals of valuation.
  • How to follow the markets and the news, and how a development in one place moves a stock in another.
  • A session on the Bloomberg Terminal.

FICC

Fixed income, currencies and commodities, across four desks. Pick one.

Oil

Analyse oil market shocks and explore the factors that drive oil prices.

30-week project
Oil trading research in R and Python: investigate what drives oil markets, and build a research platform to test trading ideas and assess their risks.
Your market
Each analyst covers Brent, WTI or a refined product, researching its price drivers, presenting bi-weekly updates and developing trade strategies.
Weeks 1–4
Understanding the market: crude vs refined products, Brent and WTI, the players involved, OPEC+, economic activity and geopolitics.
Week 5 onwards
Analysts are assigned their oil market, formulate trading strategies and begin trading.

Metals

Precious and industrial metals, and the macro forces behind them.

Precious
Gold, silver and platinum: investment, jewellery and safe-haven demand.
Industrial
Copper, aluminium, cobalt and nickel: construction, manufacturing and technology.
Macro drivers
Dollar strength, inflation (PPI), PMIs, China and the global economy, and central bank decisions.
How we trade
Futures contracts on the LME and COMEX, with long and short positions.
Themes
The energy transition’s demand for silver and copper, data-centre spending, China’s rare-earth export restrictions and US metal tariffs.
Markets
A closer look at how commodity markets have developed.

Fixed Income

From pricing a single bond to running a book. Gilts, Treasuries, swaps, linkers, central banks and credit.

Price a bond
Cash flows, yield to maturity, duration and convexity.
Read the curve
What the shape of the yield curve says, 2s10s, and how the BoE and Fed move it.
Research
Model-driven: use Python models to test trading ideas and explain what the data shows.
Run the book
Trade with your research.
Case studies
Burry’s subprime short in 2008, and the LDI gilt crisis in 2022.

G10 FX

G10 FX spot trading against the US dollar.

1–3 months
Tactical trades built on what the market has priced, where we expect data surprises, positioning and sentiment.
6–12 months
Structural views from fundamentals such as real rate gaps, the balance of payments and valuation (PPP).
Week 5 onwards
Publish our own bank-style FX reports, edited every two weeks.

Macro

Learn what’s driving different markets, and how to trade it.

Form a view on the world, then express it across asset classes. G10 or emerging markets, decided by analyst preference.

Portfolios
Run two mock portfolios on Interactive Brokers (IBKR).
Views
Generate a view through macro reports and express it across asset classes and products.
Skills
Learn how to digest the market and structure cross-asset trades.
Debates
Meet regularly and debate throughout the year.

Quant

Systematic trading and research: maths, statistics and code.

Build models, price options and test ideas with data. And get ready for the online assessments quant firms set.

Skills
Python, C++, Git and LaTeX, for systematic trading and research.
Maths and statistics
Linear algebra, vector calculus and statistics, applied to markets.
Projects
Synthetic implied-volatility models and options pricing.
Learning
Statistical modelling vs machine learning, the bias–variance trade-off and PAC methods.
Interviews
Interview preparation, with a focus on online assessments.

Who runs each division

Every division is led by its own division heads, who set the research and run the books.
