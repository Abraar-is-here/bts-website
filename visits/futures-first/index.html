Futures First, Swansea

An in-person visit to Futures First’s Swansea trading floor on Thursday 22 October, for the 2026/27 analyst cohort. Read the details, then register your interest at the end of the page: places are limited, and we’ll email you if you’re selected.

For BTS analysts only. Please don’t share this link.

Thursday 22 October, around 11:30 AM to 3:30 PMFinal timings to be confirmed

The visit

  • An introduction to Futures First and the markets it trades
  • A tour of the trading floor
  • Meeting and networking with traders and recent graduates
  • Hands-on time on their trading simulators
  • Food and refreshments

It’s a rare chance to see a trading firm from the inside, and it could lead to a Spring Insight or Summer Internship, so we hope you’ll make the effort to come.

Getting there

Getting there and back is your own responsibility, and travel costs aren’t covered or reimbursed.

  • Train: GWR from Bristol Temple Meads to Swansea, about 2 hours direct.
  • Coach: FlixBus from Bristol Bus & Coach Station, about 2 hours 50 minutes, from around £8.
  • Either way, the office is a 15-minute walk from the station.

Fares and times change, so check before you book.

About Futures First

In its own words, “a technology-enabled services firm operating in the domain of global derivatives markets”, analysing futures and options across asset classes on exchanges around the world. Swansea is one of its offices, alongside London, Montréal, Warsaw and Dubai.
