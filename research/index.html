The standard

Fixed before the first entry

  1. Stamped at entry

    Instrument, level and date, recorded when the call is made. Not edited after.

  2. Reviewed at six months

    The author returns and writes what happened and what they misread.

  3. The misses stay up

    A thesis that broke is not removed. The review of it is the point.

  4. Named

    Every piece carries its author and their year.
