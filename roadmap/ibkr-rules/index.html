BTS Trading Rules: IBKR

1. The basics

  • Use either the desktop app or IBKR Trader Workstation (TWS) – The learning curve on TWS is steeper, so choose wisely on what you’d want to use throughout the year.
  • Stick with the $1m in start money for the paper account.
  • No resets, top-ups or transfers between accounts once you’ve begun trading.
  • Results are published on the BTS website, including positions and every trade. Don’t worry about reporting, we’d just need the exports each week.

2. What we trade for

We want directional views and catalyst-driven trades built on research. Think about what the market has priced, what will change it, and when. I’m not ruling out day-trading but would sway against it.

There is no minimum or maximum holding period, and no minimum number of trades. A position can last hours, days, weeks or months. Short-term trades are allowed, but every trade still needs a thesis. Waiting for a good idea is fine; trading for the sake of it is not.

3. Division heads are responsible for portfolio performance – please do not blame your analysts for bad ideas.

4. What each desk can trade

  • Stay in your market. Trade mainly within your desk’s mandate. For example, the EU Equities desk would stick to largely the Euro Stoxx 600. You wouldn’t focus on trading, say, Bund yields.
  • However – hedges and related instruments are allowed outside your market if the pitch explains why they are the right way to express or protect the view.
  • Allowed: long and short positions, futures and options.
  • Not allowed: crypto and CFDs. I’m not hearing anyone wanting to trade Solana.
  • Options: buy options or trade defined-risk spreads only. No uncovered short options, because their loss cannot be capped.

5. Risk limits

The dollar figures below are at the starting $1,000,000.

LimitRuleAt $1,000,000
Total exposureGross exposure (longs plus shorts) of no more than 200% of account value$2,000,000
Single positionNo more than 25% of account value$250,000
Loss per tradePlanned maximum loss of no more than 2% of account value$20,000
Desk drawdownDown 15% from the account’s peak: no new risk until the desk has reviewed with its division head$150,000 from the peak
  • Futures count at full contract value. A full-size contract can breach the 25% limit on its own (an E-mini S&P or COMEX gold contract, for example), so check the contract value first and use micro contracts where needed.
  • Options count at the premium paid. On a bought option or defined-risk spread, the most you can lose counts towards the 2% limit.
  • If a limit is breached, reverse your trades by the end of the trading day.

6. Conduct

  • Only the division head and analysts use the desk’s account login. Do not share it.
  • Trade only on public information. This goes without saying – if you somehow get access to a firm’s alpha, do not trade on it.
  • Do not change or leave out trades to improve how the desk looks.
  • The whole purpose is learning for both division heads and analysts – do not beat yourself up over the numbers.

7. Reporting

Instructions on what’s required for exporting performance metrics will be shared shortly.

8. How desks are judged

Desks are reviewed on three things together:

  1. Return: how much the desk made.
  2. Risk: how it made it, including Sharpe ratio and drawdown.
  3. Process: the quality of pitches, whether trades followed their plan, and how well the desk explains its wins and losses – this will be largely reflected through the trading competition and bi-weekly meetings.
