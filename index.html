Oliver Wyman in Bristol

Oliver Wyman, a global strategy consulting firm, is hosting two events in Bristol on Wednesday 21 October: a panel open to every student, and a dinner for a small group chosen from CVs.

  • Wed 21 October 5:00 – 6:00 PM

    Consulting Panel & Q&A

    Fry Building, LG.02. What Oliver Wyman does and what a career in strategy consulting looks like, then an open Q&A. Open to all students, no application needed.

  • Wed 21 October From 7:00 PM

    CV-Screened Dinner

    A small group of students dines with Oliver Wyman consultants and partners at a restaurant in Bristol. Submit your CV by 8:00 PM on Friday 16 October. CVs are reviewed on a rolling basis, so apply early.
