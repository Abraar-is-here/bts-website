Sponsors & Partners

The firms we work with

We partner with trading firms, banks and platforms who want direct access to students who are already doing the work. Below is who we work with, and what a partnership with the Bristol Trading Society actually provides.

Our reach

We send more members into industry than any other finance society at Bristol.

800 members across the University of Bristol
40–50 analysts selected each year through a competitive application process
4 divisions — FICC, Equities, Macro and Quant — each running its own research agenda

What a partnership provides

Access to the analyst programme

Our analysts are selected through a competitive process and spend the year producing research in a specific division. Partners can receive the analyst CV book directly.

Speaking slots and events

Host an evening with our members, run a workshop, or co-host a competition. Past partners have run simulations, championships and career sessions with us.

Division-level collaboration

Work directly with the division closest to your desk — providing resources, data or problem sets to the FICC, Equities, Macro or Quant teams.

Brand presence

Logo placement across our site and event materials, and visibility at the Freshers' Fair and our annual welcome event.

Recruitment pipeline

Advertise internships, spring weeks and graduate roles to a membership that is already actively applying into markets roles.

A long-term relationship

Several of our partnerships have run for multiple years, carrying over as each committee hands to the next.
