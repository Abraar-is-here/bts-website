Sponsorship · 2026/27

Partner with us

Our analyst cohort is selected by competitive application, then spends the year producing and defending research. By the time your firm meets them, the filtering is done.

We send more members into industry than any other finance society at Bristol.

Partnership tiers

Principal

Principal Partner

Full access to the analyst cohort, your name on a flagship event, and first choice of dates in the recruiting calendar.

From

£4,000

per academic year

Talent

  • The analyst CV book — the full cohort and division heads, organised by division, sent direct to your team.
  • A closed evening with the analyst cohort — the selected analysts and division heads only, not the general membership.
  • Judging seats at the Annual Trading Competition — seven teams pitch and defend under questioning, so you spend an evening watching candidates think on their feet.

Position

  • Named sponsor of a flagship event — the Annual Trading Competition, the Macro Debate or a simulation, subject to availability.
  • First pick of the calendar — Principal Partners choose dates before anyone else, so your session lands ahead of the spring-week cycle rather than after it.
  • Events on your terms — speaker evenings, workshops, technical sessions or case studies, as many as your calendar allows.

Presence

  • Primary logo — society fleece, website, event materials and our Freshers’ Fair stand.
  • Division-level collaboration — set a research question, share data, or put your desk in front of the division closest to it.
  • Roles posted directly — spring weeks, internships and graduate schemes to the full membership.
Associate

Associate Partner

The CV book and a session with our members, without the flagship commitment.

From

£2,500

per academic year

Talent

  • The analyst CV book — the full cohort and division heads, organised by division.
  • Invitations to the Annual Trading Competition — attend, meet the teams and watch them present.

Presence

  • Events with our members — speaker evenings and workshops, scheduled around the calendar.
  • Logo — society fleece, website and event materials.
  • Collaboration with one division — the division closest to your desk.
  • Roles posted directly — spring weeks, internships and graduate schemes to the full membership.

Something else in mind? These are starting points, not fixed packages. Both tiers are open to as many firms as want them, and everything scales — a single event, a multi-year arrangement, or something built around a specific hiring need. Tell us what you are trying to achieve on a call and we will price it around that.

What partners have run with us

  • DRW — a closed evening for our analysts and division heads.
  • SIGMA Financial — their CEO to a full lecture theatre on trading and AI tooling.
  • Standard Chartered — an evening with their Head of Macro Trading, AME.
  • AmplifyME — a live portfolio simulation running $20m. Sold out, then ran again by demand.
  • TP ICAP — their Global Head of Compliance Technology on data and liquidity.
  • WorldQuant — competition routes and resources for our Quant division.

How it works

  1. You email us

    Tell us roughly what you are hiring for. We reply within two working days.

  2. A call

    Half an hour to work out what is actually useful to you, and what it costs.

  3. Dates in the diary

    We agree the year up front, so your sessions land when they matter to your cycle.

  4. One point of contact

    A named committee member runs it end to end. You are not chasing students.

Firms we work with

Starting a partnership

Email us at bristol-trading-society@bristol.ac.uk and we will come back to you within two working days. Partnerships run September to June; the earlier a firm commits, the more of the calendar is still open.
