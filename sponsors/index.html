Sponsorship · 2026/27

Partner with the Bristol Trading Society

We are a student-run trading and markets society at the University of Bristol. Partners work with us because our analyst cohort is selected, trained and producing research all year — so by the time a firm meets them, the filtering is already done.

This page is our brochure. Everything a partnership includes, and what it costs, is set out below.

Who you would be reaching

We send more members into industry than any other finance society at Bristol.

800 members across the University of Bristol
400+ applications a year to the analyst programme
7 analyst teams across four divisions
14 division head positions
2017 founded, and student-run every year since

The four divisions — FICC, Equities, Macro and Quant — run as seven teams, each with its own research agenda and its own division heads. Members go on to Goldman Sachs, Millennium, Citi, Santander CIB, Standard Chartered and Deloitte among others — see the alumni directory and how the society is structured.

Sponsorship tiers

Two tiers, both running the full academic year from September to June.

Headline Partner

£4,000 / year

For firms that want their name on the year and first access to the analyst cohort. Limited to one firm per cycle.

  • Title sponsor of the Annual Trading Competition — our flagship event, named for your firm, with judging seats for your team.
  • The analyst CV book — CVs of the analyst cohort and division heads, sent directly to you.
  • Two events of your choosing — a speaker evening, a workshop, a masterclass or a technical session.
  • An invite-only evening with the analyst cohort — the selected analysts and division heads, not the general membership.
  • Primary logo placement — society fleece, website, and all event materials.
  • Priority on dates — first pick of the calendar, so your session is not competing with another firm.
  • Division-level collaboration — set problem sets, share data, or work with a division on a live research question.
  • Recruitment postings — spring weeks, internships and graduate roles to the full membership.

Division Partner

£2,500 / year

For firms that want a strong recruiting presence and a real session with members, without the headline commitment.

  • The analyst CV book — CVs of the analyst cohort and division heads.
  • One event — a speaker evening or a workshop with our members.
  • Logo placement — society fleece, website, and event materials.
  • Collaboration with one division — the division closest to your desk.
  • Recruitment postings — spring weeks, internships and graduate roles to the full membership.
  • Invitations to the Annual Trading Competition — attend and meet the teams.

Something else in mind? These are starting points, not fixed packages. If you want a single event, a multi-year arrangement, or something built around a specific hiring need, email bristol-trading-society@bristol.ac.uk and we will put something together.

What partners have done with us

  • DRW — an invite-only evening for our analysts and division heads.
  • SIGMA Financial — their CEO spoke to a full lecture theatre on trading and AI tooling, as part of an ongoing partnership.
  • Standard Chartered — an evening with their Head of Macro Trading, AME. The discussion ran well past the scheduled finish.
  • AmplifyME — a live portfolio-management simulation running $20m of capital. Sold out, then ran again by demand.
  • TP ICAP — their Global Head of Compliance Technology on the data and liquidity side of the industry.
  • Outlook Energy — our commodities partner brings BTS members to Energy Trading Week each year.
