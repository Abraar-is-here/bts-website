Events and Speakers

What we do across the year

The Bristol Trading Society runs a year-round programme built around one idea: members learn markets by doing the work, then presenting it to people who do it professionally. Below are the events we run every year.

Our flagship competition

Annual Trading Competition

Our flagship event. Seven teams each pitch an instrument from their own division — equities, commodities, rates and macro — and defend it in front of external judges drawn from industry.

The 2025–26 edition was won by the US 10-year yield and Diageo teams, with pitches spanning Brent crude, copper, Eli Lilly, Novo Nordisk and European defence.

  • Annual
  • 7 teams
  • External judges
  • Stock-pitch format

Competitions & simulations

Annual

Macro Debate Competition

A for-and-against debate on a live macro question. The 2025–26 motion asked whether Argentina should adopt a free-floating exchange rate; the "for" side won.

With AmplifyME

AmplifyME Trading Simulator

A live portfolio-management simulation run with AmplifyME — members manage $20m of capital through a compressed year of market moves, news and research. Our first event sold out, and a second followed by demand.

Open to all

Bloomberg Terminal Sessions

Hands-on give-it-a-go sessions on the Bloomberg Terminal, open to members with no prior experience.
