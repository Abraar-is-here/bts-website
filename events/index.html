Flagship

The Annual Trading Competition

Seven teams. One instrument each. External judges.

Every division — equities, commodities, rates and macro — puts up a team. Each pitches a single instrument and then defends it under questioning from judges drawn from industry.

The 2025–26 edition was won by the US 10-year yield and Diageo teams, from a field spanning Brent crude, copper, Eli Lilly, Novo Nordisk and European defence.

Competitions and simulations

With AmplifyME

AmplifyME Financial Accelerator

A live portfolio-management simulation. Members manage $20m of capital through a compressed year of market moves, news and research, and are ranked on what they do with it. Our first event sold out; a second followed by demand.

Next session, 22 September →

Annual

Macro Debate Competition

A for-and-against debate on a live macro question, argued in front of the room. The 2025–26 motion asked whether Argentina should adopt a free-floating exchange rate — the “for” side took it.

Open to all · no experience needed

Bloomberg Terminal Sessions

Hands-on give-it-a-go sessions on the Terminal. The fastest way to go from never having touched market data to pulling a curve yourself.
