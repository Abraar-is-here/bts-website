UK Investment Competition 2026

With UMushroom

Seven weeks against students from universities across the UK. Run a £100,000 virtual portfolio on your own or in a team of up to three, scored equally on performance and on what you learn along the way. The top ten at each university go through to an elimination round, and the winners present their strategy to a jury of financial professionals at the Grand Final in London.

The Annual Trading Competition

All 13 teams. One instrument each. External judges.

This year every analyst team competes: each runs a mock portfolio, pitches a single instrument and defends it under questioning from judges drawn from industry.

Last year’s seven-team edition was won by the US 10-year yield and Diageo teams, from a field spanning Brent crude, copper, Eli Lilly, Novo Nordisk and European defence.

  • 13 teams

Competitions and simulations

With AmplifyME

AmplifyME Pathways launch

A live portfolio-management simulation. Members manage $20m of capital through a compressed year of market moves, news and research, and are ranked on what they do with it. Our first event sold out; a second followed by demand.

How the Pathways launch ran →

Annual

Macro Debate Competition

A for-and-against debate on a live macro question, argued in front of the room. The 2025–26 motion asked whether Argentina should adopt a free-floating exchange rate — the “for” side took it.

Open to all · no experience needed

Bloomberg Terminal Sessions

Hands-on give-it-a-go sessions on the Terminal. The fastest way to go from never having touched market data to pulling a curve yourself.
