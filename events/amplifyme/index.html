Financial Accelerator

A live trading simulation run by AmplifyME, and the launch of Pathways — their platform for building assessed, practical experience across markets, banking and quant through the rest of the year.

The launch of Pathways

This session doubles as the launch of AmplifyME Pathways, their platform for building practical experience across markets, banking and quant. Rather than one workshop and nothing after it, Pathways is a structured route that runs through the year and gets harder as you go.

You start with on-demand foundations, move up through live workshops built around problems that desks actually face, and finish each track with a careers masterclass and live Q&A.

The three Pathways tracks and their levels

0 On demand · 1 hr Single stock trading Introduction to modelling Intro to quant
1 Live · 2 hrs Portfolio management Three statement modelling Introduction to Python
2 Live · 2 hrs Hedge fund trading strategies Valuation modelling Quantitative market making

Why it is worth turning up for

The simulations are assessed. That is the part most people miss: what you do in the room is scored, and the score follows you.

  • Free access, for everyone. Every University of Bristol student gets Pathways at no cost. Most universities pay per student licence.
  • Assessed performance. Every simulation is marked, and you can improve your score by repeating them across the term.
  • Referrals to firms. AmplifyME invite top performers to their High Potential Candidate programme, which refers students to partner firms including Morgan Stanley. They say they only ever pass on positive referrals.
  • Evidence for your CV. Performance data feeds a finance CV builder, and each completed track carries a certificate accredited by the London Institute of Banking & Finance.
  • Leaderboards. Individual and university-level, so you can see where you sit — and where Bristol sits against everyone else.
  • Deadlines in one place. An application tracker pre-loaded with internship and graduate deadlines.

The simulation on the day

AmplifyME’s Financial Accelerator puts you on a simulated trading floor. You are given a desk, a book and live market conditions, and you trade them. Everyone in the room is doing the same thing at the same time, so you can see immediately where your reasoning held and where it did not.

No prior experience is needed and nothing is assumed. If you have never placed a trade in your life, this is the session that shows you what the job actually involves.

  • A briefing on the instruments and the scenario before you start.
  • Live simulated trading, in teams, against the rest of the room.
  • A debrief on what moved, what worked, and why.
  • A guest speaker from industry — to be confirmed.
