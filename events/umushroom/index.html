The competition, in short

Seven weeks against students from LSE, Imperial, UCL, Warwick and others across the UK. You run a £100,000 virtual portfolio, on your own or in a team of up to three, and you are scored equally on how it performs and on what you learn along the way. The top ten at each university go through to an elimination round, and the winners present their strategy to a jury of financial professionals at the Grand Final in London.

  • Starts 12 October 2026, with the performance freeze on 20 November.
  • Grand Final 1 December 2026, in London.
  • Entry £12 per student, in teams of one to three.
  • Prizes internships, coaching and vouchers.
