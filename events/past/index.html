2026–27

The year so far. Each event lands here once it finishes.

  1. 1Dec 2026 UK Investment Competition 2026 With UMushroom · 12 October to 1 December Seven weeks running a £100,000 virtual portfolio, alone or in a team of up to three, with the final in London. The competition →
  2. 8Oct 2026 J.P. Morgan: Networking Brunch & Markets Workshop With J.P. Morgan · on campus A markets trading game and a fireside chat over brunch.
  3. 7Oct 2026 J.P. Morgan: An Evening with Markets Panel With J.P. Morgan · on campus Bristol alumni from Sales, Trading, Research and Structuring, then a networking reception.
  4. 29Sep 2026 UK Investment Competition: the briefing call With UMushroom · online Dr. Luba Schoenig of UMushroom on how the competition runs and how to enter for Bristol. Details →
  5. 24Sep 2026 Annual Welcome Event Pugsley Lecture Theatre The society, the four divisions and the analyst programme, introduced to new members. It filled Pugsley Lecture Theatre.
  6. 22Sep 2026 AmplifyME Pathways launch With AmplifyME A live portfolio-management simulation: $20m of capital through a compressed year of markets. The first session sold out and a second followed. How it ran →

2025–26

Last year’s flagships.

  1. Apr – Jun2026 Annual Trading Competition Seven teams · external judges Each analyst team ran a mock portfolio and pitched one instrument. The US 10-year yield and Diageo teams won, from a field of Brent crude, copper, Eli Lilly, Novo Nordisk and European defence. The pitches →
  2. Spring2026 Macro Debate Competition Annual The motion: should Argentina adopt a free-floating exchange rate? The “for” side took it.
