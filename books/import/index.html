Publish your book

For division heads. Export your IBKR statement, drop it below, check what it shows, then send the file it gives you to the web team. Your export stays on this computer: nothing is uploaded until you send it.

See the books →

  1. 1 Set up the export Once, about five minutes.
    1. Log in to the IBKR Portal with your paper account (the one your book trades in).
    2. Go to Performance & Reports › Flex Queries. Under Activity Flex Query, press + to create one. Call it BTS books.
    3. Tick these sections. In each one, choose Select All for the fields, then Save:
      • Net Asset Value (NAV) in Base: the daily value of the account. Returns, Sharpe and drawdown come from this.
      • Open Positions: options Summary.
      • Trades: options Execution. This brings each fill with IBKR’s realised P&L and its Order Reference.
      • Cash Transactions: options Deposits & Withdrawals, so a reset or top-up isn’t counted as a return.
      Leave Account Information unticked: the books don’t need your name or address.
    4. Under Delivery Configuration: Format XML, Period Last 30 Calendar Days.
    5. Under General Configuration: Date Format yyyyMMdd, Time Format HHmmss, Date/Time Separator ; (semi-colon). Leave the rest as it is, then Continue and Create.

    Credit your analysts. When an analyst places an order, they type their initials in the order ticket’s Order Ref field (in TWS it is on the order ticket’s Misc tab). IBKR carries it through to the export, and the trade log shows who made each trade.

  2. 2

    Every Friday after the close, run it: Flex Queries › BTS books › the run arrow › XML › Run, and download the file. IBKR keeps only about 45 days of statements for paper accounts, so never leave more than four weeks between exports.

  3. 3

    Choose your book and drop the file here. The published history is loaded and your export is added to it.

Check it

    Trade notes

    Optional. Who made the trade and the idea behind it, in a line. Shown in the trade log under the instrument.

    Send it

    Download the file, then email it to the web team, or upload it to the site’s books/data folder on GitHub if you have access. Your book updates on the site within a few minutes.
