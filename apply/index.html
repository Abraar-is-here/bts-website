Committee applications · 2026/27

Four committee positions are open

We are expanding the committee. Over the last year the society has grown into an analyst programme of seven teams, a mentorship scheme, and partnerships with trading firms, banks and platforms. The next stage is doing that at a larger scale — more firms, better events, and a public presence that matches the standard of the work.

These two roles exist because that growth is currently carried by people who are already running something else. Both are real jobs with real ownership, not titles.

The roles

2 positions

Marketing & Media Officer

You own how the society looks from the outside — to students deciding whether to join, and to firms deciding whether to work with us.

What you would do

  • Run our Instagram and LinkedIn day to day — planning, posting, and replying.
  • Cover events. Photos, short video and write-ups from competitions, speaker evenings and socials, turned into content while it is still current.
  • Keep our public presence accurate: the website, our LinkedIn page and our Students’ Union listing.
  • Build the pre-term push that fills the Welcome Event and the analyst intake.
  • Work alongside our existing marketing lead rather than in isolation.

What we are looking for

  • Consistency over flair. Posting every week beats posting brilliantly once a term.
  • Judgement about what looks professional. Our audience includes people who recruit for a living.
  • Some comfort with design or editing tools — Canva, CapCut, Lightroom or similar. Useful, not essential.
  • Willingness to actually be at events with a camera.

2 positions

Outreach & Operations Officer

You make things happen: the rooms are booked, and the firms we want to hear from have actually been contacted.

What you would do

  • Own room bookings and event logistics with the Students’ Union and the University, far enough ahead that we get the rooms we want.
  • Write and send cold outreach to trading firms, banks and platforms — the top of our sponsorship pipeline.
  • Keep a record of who has been contacted, when, and what was said, so a conversation does not die when a committee changes over.
  • Follow up. Most first emails get no reply; the value is almost entirely in the second and third.
  • Support delivery on the day — setup, sign-in, making sure a visiting speaker is looked after.

What we are looking for

  • Comfort writing to a stranger at a firm and not being put off by silence.
  • Organisation you can prove. Bookings, deadlines and a pipeline that is genuinely maintained.
  • Care with wording. You would sometimes be the first thing a firm reads from us.
  • Discretion around partner conversations and anything commercial.

You can apply for both. Rank them on the form and we will consider you for each. No prior committee experience is expected for either role.
