Outreach & Operations Officer

You make things happen: the rooms are booked, and the firms we want to hear from have actually been contacted.

The role

  • Own room bookings and event logistics with the SU and the University.
  • Write and send cold outreach to trading firms, banks and platforms.
  • Keep a record of who was contacted and when, so nothing dies at handover.
  • Follow up. Most first emails get no reply; the value is in the second.

What we look for

  • Comfortable cold emailing firms you have never spoken to, and not put off by silence.
  • Organised. Bookings, deadlines and a pipeline that is genuinely maintained.
  • Care with wording. You are sometimes the first thing a firm reads from us.
  • Discretion around anything commercial.
